Strategic Planning & Curriculum Design

  • Market Needs Assessment Surveying regional employers to identify specific skill gaps.
  • Curriculum Accreditation Aligning courses with national and international regulatory bodies.
  • Course Structure Defining certification tracks for technical, vocational, and digital arts.
  • Evaluation Framework Embedding quality checks like Kirkpatrick’s four levels of training evaluation.

Infrastructure & Resource Procurement

  • Workshop Construction Building dedicated learning environments for hands-on, practical trades.
  • Tech Infrastructure Equipping computer labs with high-speed internet, servers, and modern hardware.
  • Learning Resources Compiling textbooks, software licenses, and physical raw training materials.
  • Sanitation & Administration Developing staff offices, secure storage facilities, and accessible public areas.

Operations & Stakeholder Partnerships

  • Trainer Capacity Building Upskilling instructors on modern teaching methodologies and technologies.
  • Industry Partnerships Collaborating with businesses for apprentice placement and guest lectures.
  • Incubation & Entrepreneurship Providing business start-up kits and mentorship spaces for fresh graduates.
